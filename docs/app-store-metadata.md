## Description

TenLing is a free Markdown editor for focused writing, reading, and presenting.

Write with a visual editor, format headings and lists, add links and tables, and find text quickly. Follow your document outline and switch to presentation mode when it is time to share your ideas.

Open and save Markdown documents on your device. Export your work to HTML or Word, and use PDF export on Mac. A compact toolbar and adaptive layout keep your writing accessible on iPhone, iPad, and Mac.

TenLing works with local files. No account, subscription, advertising, or analytics is required. Choose a light or dark appearance and keep your focus on the page.

## Owner-approved privacy and content declarations

The app does not collect data or track users. Documents stay on the device unless the user chooses a file location that syncs through their own file provider. No third-party content is bundled beyond licensed open-source dependencies. Users open and edit their own documents; TenLing has no public content feed, messaging service, gambling, advertising, or in-app purchases.
